## Page 1
Executive summary. This CMR report sizes the market for public and private electric vehicle charging equipment, installation and network services.

## Page 6
The global EV charging infrastructure market was valued at USD 25.3 billion in 2024 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 22.4% from 2025 to 2030, driven by government charger-rollout programmes and falling hardware costs.

## Page 10
DC fast charging is the fastest growing segment, while home AC charging remains the largest by unit count.
