## Page 1
Executive summary. This CMR report covers residential water purifiers, including point-of-use RO and UV units and whole-house filtration systems. Rising concern about water quality and growing urban household incomes drive demand.

## Page 5
The global residential water purifier market was valued at USD 38.4 billion in 2024 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 7.1% from 2025 to 2030. Asia-Pacific accounted for 47% of 2024 revenue.

## Page 9
Channels. Online sales are growing faster than retail, while service contracts and filter replacements provide recurring revenue for leading brands.
