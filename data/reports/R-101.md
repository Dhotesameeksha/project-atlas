## Page 1
Executive summary. This report from Cognitive Market Research (CMR) sizes the global smart water meter market and forecasts its growth through 2030. Utilities are replacing mechanical meters with connected devices to cut non-revenue water, automate billing and detect leaks earlier.

## Page 3
Scope and definitions. The market covers AMR and AMI smart water meters (ultrasonic, electromagnetic and mechanical meters with smart registers), communication modules, meter data management software, and installation and managed services. Revenue is reported at the manufacturer and service-provider level in current USD.

## Page 8
The global smart water meter market was valued at USD 4.21 billion in 2024 and is expected to grow at a CAGR of 11.2% from 2025 to 2030. Asia-Pacific accounted for 38% of 2024 revenue.

## Page 9
Regional view. Asia-Pacific leads because of large municipal rollouts in China, India and Japan, followed by Europe and North America where replacement cycles dominate. Latin America and the Middle East and Africa remain early-stage but are growing from a small base.

## Page 12
Methodology note. The base year for all market size estimates is 2024. The forecast period is 2025 to 2030. This report does not publish a separate market size for 2025; readers who need a single-year figure for 2025 or later must derive it from the 2024 base and the stated CAGR.

## Page 15
Competitive landscape. The market is moderately concentrated. Leading vendors compete on battery life, communication protocol support and total cost of ownership, and increasingly bundle analytics software with hardware.
